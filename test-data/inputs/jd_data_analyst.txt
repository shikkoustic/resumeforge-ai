Data Analyst Intern

About the role
Join our analytics team to turn raw sales and customer data into reports the business
actually uses.

Requirements
- Strong SQL, including joins, window functions and aggregation
- Python with pandas for data cleaning and analysis
- Experience building dashboards in Tableau or Power BI
- Advanced Excel including pivot tables and lookups
- Ability to present findings clearly to non-technical stakeholders

Good to have
- Exposure to basic statistics or A/B testing
- Familiarity with a cloud data warehouse

What you will do
- Write SQL to answer business questions
- Build and maintain weekly reporting dashboards
- Automate manual reporting processes
