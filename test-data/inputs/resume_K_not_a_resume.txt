Rajma Chawal Recipe

Soak 1 cup rajma overnight. Pressure cook with salt for 6 whistles. In a pan heat 2 tbsp
oil, add cumin, chopped onions and cook till golden. Add ginger garlic paste, tomato
puree, turmeric, red chilli powder and garam masala. Add the cooked rajma with its water
and simmer for 20 minutes. Garnish with coriander and serve hot with steamed rice.
